SKETCH3D is a touch-first parametric 3D sketch modeler for iPad, Apple Pencil, and desktop web browsers. Draw 2D closed polygon profiles with 1mm/5mm/10mm grid snapping, drop calibrated metric screw holes (M2 2.2mm, M3 3.2mm, M4 4.2mm, M5 5.2mm with +0.2mm FDM clearance), extrude up to 256mm, bevel edges, apply axial twist, and export watertight binary STL files sized for the Bambu Lab A1 3D printer.

SKETCH3D Quick Start & Guide

3-Step Workflow

  1. Pick a starter or tap to draw: Place points on the 256×256 mm grid. Tap any edge to insert new control points.
  2. Parametric modifiers: Drag Extrude Z (1–256 mm), Bevel (0–4 mm), and Twist (0–180°).
  3. Export .STL: Download slicer-ready binary STL with printer Z-up coordinates for Bambu Studio or Bambu Handy.
Bambu Lab A1 Target 256 × 256 × 256 mm build volume, standard 0.4 mm nozzle, textured PEI plate. Live printability guardrails check bed limits.
Metric Screw Clearances Pre-calibrated M2 (2.2 mm), M3 (3.2 mm), M4 (4.2 mm), and M5 (5.2 mm) diameter presets including +0.2 mm FDM tolerance.
Tip: Click the ⊙ + Hole button to toggle hole mode. When in hole mode, click the button again to cycle between M2, M3, M4, and M5.
