About SKETCH3D — Parametric Browser CAD for Bambu Lab A1

SKETCH3D is a touch-first, zero-install 2D-to-3D parametric sketch modeler engineered specifically for 3D printing workflows on the Bambu Lab A1 ($256 \times 256 \times 256\text{ mm}$ build envelope). It runs 100% client-side in iPad Safari (with full Apple Pencil support) and modern desktop browsers, translating planar 2D sketches into watertight, slicer-ready binary STL files with no cloud roundtrips or backend server required.

1. 2D Sketching & Live Dimensions

Draw closed outer profiles directly on a millimeter grid scaled to the Bambu A1 bed. Use multi-step grid snap (1 mm, 5 mm, 10 mm, or Off), drag vertices to reshape, tap outer edges to insert new control points, and monitor live millimeter edge length labels.

2. Metric Screw Hole Clearances

Drop pre-calibrated metric screw holes: M2 (2.2 mm ∅), M3 (3.2 mm ∅), M4 (4.2 mm ∅), and M5 (5.2 mm ∅). Each preset includes a $+0.2\text{ mm}$ FDM clearance tolerance so printed hardware threads slide through cleanly without reaming.

3. Parametric Modifiers & Slicer STL

Extrude vertically up to $256\text{ mm}$, bevel perimeters ($0–4\text{ mm}$), and apply continuous helical axial twist ($0–180^\circ$). Printability guardrails warn if parts exceed bed limits or are too thin ($< 1.2\text{ mm}$). Export directly to binary STL with printer Z-up coordinates for Bambu Studio.
