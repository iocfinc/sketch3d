Recreate Three Printable Hardware Models in Under Five Minutes Each

Step-by-step visual recipes using SKETCH3D's Shapes ▾, Round, Shell, 3×2 Grid, M3 Standoff, and built-in watertight .STL pre-flight verifier for your Bambu Lab A1.

Recipe 1: Modular EDC Wallet Plate (88 × 56 × 3 mm)

Flat-pack cardholder plate with pocket-safe rounded corners, a thumb-slide cutout, and four M3 corner screw holes.

  1. Tap Clear All, open Shapes ▾ → Rectangle, and drag an 88 × 56 mm card outline.
  2. Set Round to 4 mm for smooth pocket edges.
  3. Open Shapes ▾ → + Cutout, trace a trapezoid thumb notch at the top edge, and tap Close.
  4. Open Shapes ▾ → + M3 Hole, drop 4 corner holes, set Extrude Z = 3 mm and Bevel = 0.5 mm, and click Export .STL.

Recipe 2: Perforated Enclosure Panel / 6-Key Plate (120 × 90 × 3 mm)

Uses the new 3×2 Cutout Grid and M3 Standoff tools to build modular mounting plates and switch matrices in seconds.

  1. Drag a 120 × 90 mm Rectangle and set Round = 3 mm.
  2. Open Shapes ▾ → ⊞ 3×2 Cutout Grid to stamp six 14 × 14 mm switch/vent openings automatically.
  3. Open Shapes ▾ → ◉ + M3 Standoff and tap 4 corners to place 7.2 mm bosses with 3.2 mm M3 bores.
  4. Set Extrude Z = 3 mm (or 12 mm with Shell: On for a full housing) and export .STL.

Recipe 3: Tool Tote Tray & Stacked Side Frames (180 × 110 × 24 mm)

Combines Shell hollowing on Part 1 with a multi-part + New Side Part triangle frame—verified watertight before slicing.

  1. Base Tray (Part 1): Drag a 180 × 110 mm Rectangle, set Round = 10 mm, toggle Shell: On (2.8 mm wall), and set Extrude Z = 24 mm.
  2. Side A-Frame (Part 2): Open Parts ▾ → + New Side Part, switch to ⬠ Poly, and plot a 100 × 130 mm triangle (90° Ortho snaps the base flat). Set Extrude Z = 5 mm.
  3. STL Pre-Flight Check: Click ⬇ Export .STL—SKETCH3D automatically strips degenerate triangles, grounds minZ = 0.0 mm to the PEI plate, and verifies 0 open edges before downloading.
