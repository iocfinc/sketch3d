Spiral Vase Solid Rationale

Exporting a solid watertight STL instead of a hollow mesh enables Bambu Studio and OrcaSlicer to slice with Spiral Vase mode. The slicer converts the outer boundary into a single, continuous Z-spiral without layer seams, retractions, or travel moves.

This yields completely seamless, translucent walls with 50% to 75% material savings compared to dual-wall shells.

0.4mm Nozzle Calibration

A standard 0.4mm nozzle reliably extrudes 0.65mm to 0.75mm wide perimeters when layer height is kept between 0.24mm and 0.28mm with flow rate set to 104%.
